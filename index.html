How to play

Drive the front funnel over pollen while holding your intake key / LB. The 256 mm intake opening collects pollen only; balls beside or behind the robot stay outside. Steer the chassis to aim the catapult. Your fire key / RT starts a 1.5 s animated windup, launches every loaded ball together, then takes 0.55 s to reset. Movement during windup changes the release position and heading. Range power assist adjusts launch power only. Your nectar supply key / B supplies unlocked nectar onto the field; this robot cannot intake it. Return to the red loading zone to park.

Practice rules & simulation model

AUTO is manually driven for solo practice. The robot stays fully in the red half (columns A–C) for 30 seconds; the centerline barrier releases in TELEOP. This strict practice boundary implements the requested restriction. Official G402 prohibits AUTO interference and gives each alliance priority on its own side; it does not prohibit every crossing. Movement stops during the 8-second break. Timings, scoring values, four-piece capacity, and nectar release gates follow the linked manual. Robot footprint: 390 mm wide × 400 mm long, including the front intake; 256 mm intake opening. Dimensions are drawn to field scale and used for body collisions. Wall and side-rail contacts allow steering and sliding. You can drive beneath the raised hive and both cells; only the two visible side base rails block ground movement. Flower collision uses the small outlined floor base; upper petals are decorative. Turn on Collision outlines to see the exact flower bases, side base rails, field boundary and robot footprint. The loading-zone tape is not an obstacle. The four corner dead wheels are passive supports. No opponents, ranking points, or referee penalties.

Flowers and loading zones follow official Figure 9-2. The tipping model uses 8 pollen / 3 nectar + 3 pollen / 5 nectar, with mixed loads interpolated by weight. Ball flight includes gravity, closed-face collisions, bouncing, and random spills. The hive envelope follows manual 9.6.1 (49.46 × 38.95 in, pivot height 43.95 in). This 2D model assumes the robot fits below both cell positions; side rail width is approximated as 1 in. These are game approximations, not a full mechanical model.
